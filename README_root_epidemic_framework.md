# Epidemic Preparedness Framework

## Overview
This repository brings together three interconnected research initiatives aimed at strengthening epidemic preparedness and public health response capacity in the United States. The framework integrates mathematical modeling, optimal control theory, and hybrid forecasting methods to provide practical, data-driven tools that inform vaccination strategies, intervention design, and outbreak preparedness.

The work supports the goals of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), the National Institutes of Health (NIH), and other federal agencies by ensuring that advanced mathematical and computational research contributes directly to national health security, economic resilience, and policy effectiveness.

## Repository Structure
The repository consists of three core components, each developed as an independent but complementary module:

| Folder | Project Title | Description |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| `vaccine-hesitancy-optimization` | Optimizing Vaccination Strategies and Reducing Vaccine Hesitancy | Develops models that integrate behavioral dynamics (vaccine hesitancy) with epidemic transmission, using optimal control to design equitable vaccination and outreach policies. |
| `multi-intervention-optimal-control` | Optimal Control Models for Intervention Strategies | Builds portfolio-based optimal control frameworks that jointly optimize multiple interventions—vaccination, NPIs, testing, and treatment—under uncertainty and risk. |
| `outbreak-forecasting-tools` | Outbreak Forecasting and Early Preparedness Tools | Combines mechanistic and machine learning models to create hybrid forecasts with calibrated uncertainty and early-warning triggers. |

Each subdirectory includes folders for `models`, `data`, `figures`, `docs`, and `results`, ensuring transparency and reproducibility.

## Objectives
1. **Advance Public Health Modeling** – Improve understanding of epidemic dynamics by coupling behavioral, biological, and policy-driven processes.
2. **Support Federal and State Decision-Making** – Provide open, accessible models that health agencies can adapt for operational use.
3. **Promote Open Science** – Disseminate all tools and findings via GitHub, peer-reviewed publications, and policy briefs for broad accessibility.

## How to Use
1. Clone this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/yourusername/epidemic-preparedness-framework.git
   ```
2. Navigate to any of the subprojects and follow the individual `README.md` instructions.
3. Each model can be run in MATLAB, Python, or hybrid simulation environments as indicated.

## Policy Relevance
The models developed here directly align with U.S. national priorities in epidemic preparedness and outbreak response, as articulated by the CDC’s Center for Forecasting and Outbreak Analytics (CFA) and NIH modeling initiatives. 
By openly sharing reproducible modeling tools, this framework ensures that evidence-based strategies for vaccination, intervention optimization, and early warning are available to researchers and public health authorities nationwide.
